# Silhouette and form

## Silhouette test

Fill the sprite with one opaque colour on empty background (mentally or via remap). Can you name the subject? If not, push limbs/props farther from the torso blob; carve negative space.

## Proportions at tiny sizes

- 16×16: iconic shapes, 1 feature max (hat, beak, gun).
- 32×32: readable head/torso/legs; skip finger detail.
- 64×64: room for costume folds — still cluster them.

## Edges

- Prefer long continuous runs over stair-noise.
- Selective outline: break lines where two mid-tones meet softly.
- Avoid even outline weight on every pixel of the perimeter.

## Overlap

Stack depth with value and occlusion, not with tiny internal outlines on every part.
